The Trip Worksheet

One page per trip. Fill it in with the AI's help (that's what the chapters are for), then plan from this page, not from the chat. Chats scroll away; worksheets don't. If you also use the free-travel-planner-kit printables, this worksheet is the "master" that feeds the paper binder — it's the thinking layer; the printables are the carrying layer.
