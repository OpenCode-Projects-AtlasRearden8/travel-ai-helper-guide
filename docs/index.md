# AI Travel Tips (Calmer Trips)

*A plain-English manual for using AI to plan calmer trips.*

Welcome! This guide explains how to use AI tools to take some of the stress out of
trip planning — like a friend would, step by step, never condescending. No
technical background needed.

## The one big idea

AI is a fast, tireless **drafting assistant**, not a travel agent. It writes
itineraries, packing lists, and translations in seconds — but it can never *check*
a train timetable, confirm a hotel exists, or hold a reservation. You do the
verifying. The guide is built entirely around that split:

- **AI drafts. You verify. You travel calmer.**

## The 10-minute starter

If you want one quick setup before reading further:

1. Pick **one** AI chat tool you can reach without a subscription (most major
   services have a free tier).
2. Install it on your phone **or** save the website address as a bookmark.
3. Copy the prompt from [chapter 1, step 2](01-itinerary-drafts-you-verify.md)
   and try it with a real trip you're considering.
4. Open the [verification checklist](verification-checklist.md) in another tab
   and check the three items marked ⭐ first.
5. Save your draft somewhere you can find it — a notes app, or the worksheet.

That's it. You're already ahead of most travelers.

## Safety habits to keep forever

- Verify prices, timetables, and opening hours on the **official website** — not
  in the chat.
- Use AI as a **translator**, not a negotiator, contract reader, or doctor.
- Never paste passport numbers, ticket codes, or full booking details into a
  chat, even a local one.
- Anything that "must be paid by wire transfer" or "only 2 left at this price" is
  a scam signal, whether AI found it or a human did.
