# The AI Verification Checklist

Print this, screenshot it, or keep it open in a tab. This is the manual's core
habit turned into one page: **AI drafts, you verify, you travel calmer.**

Time cost of the ⭐ items: about 15 minutes per trip. Everything else is
minutes-per-week and prevents hours of pain.

## ⭐ Before you trust an AI itinerary (chapter 1)

- [ ] Opening hours + closed days of your top 3 sights — checked on the
      **official** site (not a blog, not the chat itself)
- [ ] Any advance-booking requirement for those sights (many cap daily entries)
- [ ] First/last departure times for any transit your plan depends on
- [ ] One-restaurant check: the single place you'd be sad to miss is open on
      your night (kitchens close earlier than doors; Monday closure is real)

## ⭐ Before you pay anything (chapter 6 — 60 seconds)

- [ ] Domain is the official one (no extra hyphens, no "cheap-discount" words)
- [ ] You arrived at that site by search or memory, **not** via a chat/
      unsolicited message link
- [ ] Price sanity check done against one independent source
- [ ] Payment is on a protected channel (card / platform) — never wire, gift
      card, or crypto for a booking
- [ ] No urgency pressure ("2 left!", "expires in 10 min") — if pressured,
      stop and sleep on it

## Before you trust an AI price or schedule (chapter 3)

- [ ] Asked yourself: *did the AI see this live, or recite from memory?*
- [ ] Live price confirmed on the airline's/hotel's own site or metasearch
- [ ] Flight connection time is kind (3h+ for self-transfers with checked bags)
- [ ] Booking confirmation saved offline + confirmed once with the provider

## Before you travel on a translation (chapter 2)

- [ ] Critical sentence (allergy, medical, "where's my platform?") also exists
      **in writing**, shown to a human — not only spoken through the phone
- [ ] Knife-edge facts ("this pass covers kids", "this fare is refundable")
      confirmed at a staffed window, not by a translated sign alone

## Before you go offline (chapter 5)

- [ ] Local model tested in airplane mode at home
- [ ] Itinerary + worksheet saved in a notes app *before* losing signal
- [ ] Offline maps downloaded for city + transit regions
- [ ] Nothing personal (passport #, ticket codes, full card details) is in any
      chat, cloud or local

## After you come home (makes next trip better)

- [ ] What the AI got wrong, written down (one line per item) — next trip's
      prompt gets smarter *even faster*
- [ ] What you over- and under-packed, noted on the [worksheet](trip-worksheet.md)

---

## The three-question emergency check

If you remember nothing else, when a chat gives you a surprising fact, ask:

1. **How would it know?** (If the answer requires live data — price, hours,
   weather — verify with the primary source)
2. **What's the harm if it's wrong?** (High harm → check; low harm → 6 hours
   of slack solves it)
3. **What would the official source say?** (Go read it; that's the check)
